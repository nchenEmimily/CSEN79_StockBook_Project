# Project 1 sample script. Lines starting with # are comments.
# Build a small book: three sellers and three buyers.
SELL alice 1 100 50.30
SELL bob 2 200 50.20
SELL carol 3 150 50.20
BUY dave 4 100 50.00
BUY erin 5 300 50.10
BUY frank 6 50 50.10
BOOK
# A buy that crosses the spread: fills bob first (oldest at 50.20), then carol, then rests.
BUY gina 7 400 50.20
BOOK 3
# Cancel a resting order, then try to cancel it again.
CANCEL 6
CANCEL 6
# A market sell sweeps the bids, best price first.
MARKET SELL alice 8 500
BOOK
# A market buy larger than everything left on the ask side.
MARKET BUY dave 9 1000
# Errors.
BUY erin 5 10 49.00
SELL bob 10 0 50.00
FOO
HISTORY alice
HISTORY gina
HISTORY dave 2
HISTORY nobody
STATS
